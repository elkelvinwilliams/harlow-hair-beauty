## 5. What this venture inherits — and what it must create

**Inherits from the house:** the name · the *set apart* ethos · the brand system (type, palette, voice, motion) · the document system (compliance, privacy, pricing, booking, money) · the standards of care.

**Must create for itself:** its promise · its first offer and pricing · its own compliance and insurance · its client tools · its lead and rhythm.

> *In plain English: the house gives a new venture its name, its look and its standards on day one. The venture's job is to find its own "chair" — and prove it the way Hair did.*
