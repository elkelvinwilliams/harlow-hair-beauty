## Part A — The policy *(client-facing — edit the brackets, then publish)*

**Booking.** All appointments are booked in advance via **[your booking method — e.g.
Instagram DM / WhatsApp / Fresha]**. Your slot is confirmed once your **deposit** is received.

**Deposit.** A **[£20–£30]** non-refundable deposit secures your appointment and comes off
your final price. The balance is paid **[on the day, by cash / bank transfer]**.

**Rescheduling.** Need to move your appointment? Please give at least **[48 hours]** notice
and we'll transfer your deposit to a new date (one move per booking). Less than **[48
hours]** notice means a new deposit.

**Cancellations.** Deposits are non-refundable, as your slot is held just for you and turns
others away.

**Lateness.** Please arrive on time — braiding takes hours and late starts affect the whole
day. We allow a **[15-minute]** grace; beyond that we may need to shorten the style or
reschedule, and a late fee of **[£10]** may apply.

**No-shows.** Not arriving, or arriving more than **[30 minutes]** late without contact,
is treated as a cancellation — the deposit is kept and a new deposit is required to rebook.

**Coming prepared.** Please arrive with hair **washed, blow-dried and detangled** unless a
wash is booked. [Add-on: wash & prep **[£X]**.] Come with clean, product-free hair for the best result.

**Guests & children.** To keep the space calm, please **[keep extra guests to a minimum]**.

> *In plain English: a small deposit holds your spot and keeps both of us protected. Give me
> a couple of days' notice if life happens and we'll move it. No-shows lose the deposit —
> because that time was yours and I turned others away for it.*
