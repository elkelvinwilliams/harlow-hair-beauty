Our story

Service beyond expectation

Hagiazo was founded on one belief: that protective styling is an art form, and every head of hair deserves to be treated as something set apart.

We specialise in braids, locs and wigs for the Afro-Caribbean community — authentic technique, healthy-hair care and a calm, unhurried chair that keeps clients coming back time and again.
