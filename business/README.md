corporate document estate — just the handful of documents that carry real legal,
financial, or brand risk, kept as living records.

> **Scope decision (2026-09-06):** We chose **Option B** — keep this a website repo,

> framework is revisited only if a real trigger hits: **hiring staff, a second
> location / renting chairs to others, or seeking funding.** Until then, this stays lean.

## How these documents work

- **Living records.** Update them as things change; each has a change log.
- **Requirement tags:** **[REGULATORY]** (with the exact law/citation) · **[BEST
  PRACTICE]** (strongly recommended, not the law) · **[ASSUMPTION]** (a number or
  claim to validate). These are kept rigorously distinct.
- **Plain English.** Every regulatory line has an *"In plain English"* explanation
  beside it — never instead of it.
- **Honest status.** Nothing is marked "approved" by a role that doesn't exist yet.

> ⚠️ **This is guidance, not legal or tax advice.** Confirm licensing with **Harlow
> District Council** and tax specifics with an accountant or [gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk).
> Thresholds and rates change each tax year — verify current figures before you rely on them.
