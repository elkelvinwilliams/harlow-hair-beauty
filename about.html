About Hagiazo

To set apart

Hagiazo — to sanctify, to make holy, to set apart for a special purpose. It is the singular promise behind every appointment — from our first chair in Harlow to our studio in Tottenham today.

Our story

Loving people through their transformation

Loving people through their transformation has always been part of who I am. It led me first into therapy — sitting with people as they grew and healed — before I understood that hair was offering me the very same gift, only in a language you could see.

Friends and family didn't simply leave looking different; they left feeling different — lighter, surer, more themselves. That became the vision: to bring both worlds beneath one roof, where the inner work and the outer finally meet.

Because a chair at Hagiazo is never just a chair. From the moment you arrive to the moment you leave, you begin the journey of becoming set apart — and the transformation is never only physical. It is, as I've always believed, a renewing of the mind.

Meet Ruvimbo

Ruvimbo is the founder and heart of Hagiazo. Before the styling chair, there was the therapist's chair — and that same instinct, to care for the whole person, shapes every appointment.

Close to fifteen years on, she does more than style hair. She makes space for you to exhale, to feel seen, and to leave a little more yourself than when you arrived.

Why You'll Feel The Difference

01

Afro-Textured Mastery

Braids, locs and wigs aren't a sideline here — they're the whole craft. Your texture is understood, respected, and worked with, never against.

02

A Considered Consultation

Every appointment opens with a real conversation. We listen first, advise honestly, and shape a style around your hair, your lifestyle and you.

Healthy hair is the foundation of every style. Gentle tension, clean partings and nourishing products mean you leave protected, not just polished.
