## ✦ Braids, Cornrows & Boho Aftercare

**You've been set apart — now keep it that way.**

- **Nights:** satin scarf, bonnet or pillowcase — every night.
- **Edges:** keep it gentle, no heavy pulling. A little light scalp oil keeps you comfortable.
- **Washing:** cleanse the scalp gently every **1–2 weeks** — don't scrub; dry fully.
- **Moisture:** a light braid spray or leave-in mid-week.
- **Best before:** take down at **4–6 weeks**, before the roots start to matte.

## ✦ Locs & Retwist Aftercare

**Healthy roots, beautiful locs.**

- **First 24 hours:** keep new twists **dry**; sleep in a satin scarf so they set.
- **Retwist:** every **4–6 weeks** — don't over-twist, it thins the roots.
- **Washing:** use a **residue-free** shampoo; dry locs **completely**.
- **Moisture:** a light water-and-oil mist — supple, not greasy.

*Questions? Message us anytime. · Hagiazo Hair, Harlow*

## ✦ Wig Care

**Treat it well and it will last.**

- **Storage:** on a wig stand or in a satin bag — never balled up.
- **Lace & edges:** remove adhesive gently; keep the lace clean so it lies flat.
- **Washing:** every **8–10 wears** with cool water and gentle shampoo; air-dry on the stand.
- **Heat:** low heat on human hair; avoid heat on synthetic unless heat-friendly.
- **Underneath:** keep your own hair braided down, moisturised, and give it a breather between installs.
