**The big idea.** *Hagiazo Hair is the first baby of Hagiazo — a house of ventures, each
one set apart.* The name was always bigger than hair: *hagiazo* means **to set apart, to
sanctify, to renew**. That is a **group philosophy**, carried into every venture.

---

## 1. Brand architecture

```
          HAGIAZO                     ← the parent / the house (distinct mark: to come)
             │
   ┌─────────┼───────────── … ──────────┐
Hagiazo    Hagiazo        Hagiazo     Hagiazo …
  Hair      Homes          Beauty      (next ventures)
 (live)    (next)
```

- **Parent brand:** **HAGIAZO** — the vision, values and name the whole house shares.
- **Ventures:** always **"Hagiazo ___"** (Hagiazo Hair, Hagiazo Homes, Hagiazo Beauty …).
- **Marks:** each venture carries an **H-based monogram** (the interlocking **HH** is Hagiazo
  Hair today; others follow as **HB**, **H + the venture's initial**). The **parent mark is
  distinct and will be created later.**
  - 🔎 *To resolve when designing marks:* several ventures start with **H** (Hair, Homes,
    Homeware, Holdings), so an "H + initial" monogram will collide. Differentiate with a
    two-letter mark or the full venture word beneath the monogram.
- **The ethos line:** **"set apart"** leads, the venture follows — *set apart, and then its
  world.* Faith kept **subtle** throughout, as in Hagiazo Hair.

## 4. The principle — prove, then build the house

**Make the first baby undeniable before building the rest.** A franchise is funded and
earned by one venture that visibly *works* — full books, real reviews, a waitlist. Hair is
that proof. Keep focus there; formalise the group **only when a real trigger hits**: Venture
II goes live, or you take investment.
