Before & after the chair

Preparation & aftercare

A little care on both sides of your appointment keeps the style flawless for weeks.

How to prepare your hair

  • Clean scalp. Arrive freshly washed, with no heavy product or build-up.
  • Detangled & blow-dried. Hair should be fully dry and stretched so partings sit clean — unless you've booked a wash with us.
  • Tell us about your scalp. Any soreness, thinning or tender edges — we'll adapt the tension and the style around you.

Extending your style

  • Satin at night. A scarf, bonnet or pillowcase stops frizz and keeps edges smooth.
  • Light scalp care. Cleanse gently every 1–2 weeks; a little light oil keeps the scalp comfortable.
  • Know when to take down. Most braided styles look their best for 4–6 weeks — take them down before the roots start to matte.
