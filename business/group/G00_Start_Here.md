## Where to begin

1. **Read G01 CEO Brief** — the house, Venture I's standing, and the eight decisions only you can make.
2. **Protect the name** — G04 Trademark Guide; the search is free and takes ten minutes.
3. **Fill the venture blanks** — fields marked *to confirm* in the Hair operations: deposit amounts (05), contact email and address (03), the live status boxes (02).
4. **When Homes becomes real,** fill its Venture Charter — every line true, and it earns its own operating pack.
5. **Keep everything living.** Each document has a change log; update it as things change.

> *In plain English: run Hair brilliantly, lock the name down now, and let the house grow one proven venture at a time.*

⚠️ **Guidance, not legal or tax advice.** Confirm licensing with Harlow District Council and tax specifics with an accountant or gov.uk. Figures change each tax year — verify before you rely on them.
