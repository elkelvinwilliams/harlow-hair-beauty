**The promise.** *Hagiazo* means to set apart. Clients don't just leave looking different —
they leave feeling renewed. Therapist-trained care meets master craft: the transformation is
never only physical.

**The edge (our moat).** Most braiders compete on price and speed. Hagiazo competes on how
the chair *feels* — a calm, restorative experience few can copy.

**Services.** Boho bob braids · cornrow & Fulani braids · French curls · knotless braids ·
knotless boho braids · custom wigs *(locs & retwists, kids and add-ons to follow).*
**Price range:** roughly **£75–£185** depending on style, parting and length (braiding hair
included). **Highest-margin line:** custom wigs (~2× the braiding hourly rate).

**Channels.** Website (live) · Instagram @hagiazohair · TikTok · Google Business (to set up).
**Website:** https://elkelvinwilliams.github.io/harlow-hair-beauty/

**Where we are.** Brand, website and real pricing are live and on-theme. The operating
basics (compliance, privacy, pricing, booking, aftercare) are documented in this pack.

**This quarter's three priorities.**
1. Convert — real photos + working booking link.
2. Compliance — insurance + ICO/GDPR.
3. Visibility — Google Business Profile + client reviews.

**Grow next.** Lean into **custom wigs** as a deliberate second income engine.

**The bigger picture.** Hagiazo Hair is **venture I of Hagiazo** — a house of ventures led by
Ruvimbo & Elkelvin. Prove Hair, then build the house (Homes next). Full vision in BIZ-08.
