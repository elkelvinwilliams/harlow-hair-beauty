## Part A — Privacy Policy *(website-ready — replace the brackets)*

**Hagiazo Hair — Privacy Policy**
*Last updated: [DATE]*

**Who we are.** Hagiazo Hair ("we", "us") is a braids & locs studio based in Harlow,
Essex. For any privacy question, contact us at **[hello@hagiazohair.co.uk]**.

**What we collect.** When you enquire or book, we may collect: your **name**, **phone
number**, **email**, the **service** you want, **appointment history and notes** (e.g.
hair length, preferences, any sensitivity you tell us), and **photos** of your hair
where you agree to them.

**Why we use it, and our lawful basis.**
- To take and manage your **booking** and contact you about it — *basis: contract.*
- To keep simple **client records and aftercare notes** so we serve you well — *basis:
  legitimate interests.*
- To meet **tax and legal** obligations (e.g. records of payments) — *basis: legal obligation.*
- To show **before/after photos** on our website and social media — *basis: your consent*
  (you can say no, or withdraw it later, and we won't use your images).
- To send you occasional **updates or offers**, only if you opt in — *basis: consent.*

**Sharing.** We don't sell your data. We share it only with the tools that run the
business — for example our **booking system [e.g. Fresha]**, **payment provider [e.g.
SumUp]**, and where the law requires (e.g. HMRC). These providers process data on our behalf.

**How long we keep it.** We keep booking and client records for as long as you're a
client and for a reasonable period after, and financial records for **at least 6 years**
as tax law expects. Photos are kept only while consent stands.

**Your rights.** You can ask to **see**, **correct**, or **delete** your data, or to
**withdraw consent** (for photos/marketing) at any time — just contact us. You can also
complain to the **Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)** at ico.org.uk or 0303 123 1113.

**Storage & security.** We keep your information secure and limit access to it. Our
website is served over HTTPS.

## Part B — Consent & retention (internal)

### Photo consent  **[REGULATORY basis: UK GDPR — consent]**
Before posting any client's hair to the website, Instagram or TikTok:
- **Ask, and record a yes/no.** A quick text reply, a ticked box on the booking form, or
  a signed line is enough — keep the record.
- Let them decline with no awkwardness; it must be freely given.
- If they later ask you to take an image down, **do it** and note it.

> *In plain English: never assume you can post a client's hair. Get a clear yes first,
> keep proof, and take it down if they change their mind.*

**Suggested consent line (booking form / message):**
> "May we photograph your hair and share it on our website and social media? ☐ Yes ☐ No —
> you can change your mind any time."

### Data-retention quick guide
| Data | Keep for | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Booking & contact details | While a client + ~2 years after last visit | Service, rebooking |
| Client/aftercare notes | While a client | Continuity of care |
| **Financial records** | **At least 6 years** | **[REGULATORY]** HMRC record-keeping |
| Photos | Only while consent stands | Consent can be withdrawn |
| Marketing opt-ins | Until they unsubscribe | Consent basis |

### Good habits
☐ Lock the phone/laptop that holds client data ☐ Don't keep client info in random chat
threads ☐ Only collect what you actually need ☐ Delete on request (except records the law
makes you keep).
