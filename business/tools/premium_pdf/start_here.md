## Where to begin

1. **Read 01 Business Summary** — a minute, and you have the whole picture.
2. **Fill the blanks.** Fields marked *to confirm* are yours to set: deposit amounts (06), your contact email and address (03), and the live status boxes (02).
3. **Protect the name.** Follow **10 Trademark Guide** — the search is free and takes ten minutes.
4. **Open the Register in Excel** and start logging sellers as you find them.
5. **Keep everything living.** Each document has a change log — update it as things change.

> *In plain English: nothing here is finished for its own sake. It is a working spine for a real studio — use it, mark it, and let it grow with Hagiazo.*

⚠️ **Guidance, not legal or tax advice.** Confirm licensing with Harlow District Council and tax specifics with an accountant or gov.uk. Figures change each tax year — verify before you rely on them.
