Two parts: **(A)** a consultation routine to run before every appointment, and **(B)**
aftercare you can give clients so their style lasts and their hair stays healthy. *(The
printable client versions are in the `client/` folder: the Consultation & Intake Form and
the Aftercare Cards.)*

---

## Part A — Consultation routine *(run before you start)*

**Always:**
- Confirm the **style, length, parting size** and show a reference photo you both agree on.
- Check **scalp & hairline condition** — note any soreness, thinning or tenderness; never
  braid too tight on fragile edges.
- Ask about **allergies / sensitivities** (braiding hair, gels, oils).
- Agree the **price and time** out loud before starting.
- Take a **"before" photo** (with consent) — useful for your portfolio and for aftercare.
- Confirm **photo consent** for social media (yes/no — see BIZ-01).

**[REGULATORY] Patch test — if you ever use colour or chemical products.**
*Citation: good-practice duty of care; relevant under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 and
product manufacturer instructions.* A patch test **at least 48 hours before** any colour
service, and don't proceed if there's any reaction.

> *In plain English: a two-minute chat and a quick scalp check before you start prevents
> sore edges, surprises about price, and the wrong style. If you ever add colour, always
> patch-test two days ahead.*

## Part B — Aftercare *(give this to clients)*

### Braids, cornrows & boho (knotless, box, Fulani, French curls)
- **Nights:** wrap with a **satin scarf or bonnet**, or sleep on a **satin pillowcase**, to
  stop frizz and keep edges smooth.
- **Edges:** go easy — avoid very tight styles and heavy pulling. A little light oil on the
  scalp keeps it comfortable.
- **Washing:** cleanse the scalp gently every **1–2 weeks** with diluted shampoo or a
  cleanser; don't scrub. Let braids dry fully to avoid odour and mildew.
- **Moisture:** a light leave-in or braid spray mid-week keeps hair from drying out.
- **Lifespan:** most braided styles look their best for **4–6 weeks** — take them down
  before they start to matte at the roots.

### Locs & retwists
- **After a retwist:** keep it **dry for 24 hours** and sleep in a satin scarf so new twists
  set and hold.
- **Routine:** retwist roughly every **4–6 weeks** — over-twisting thins and stresses the
  roots, so don't overdo it.
- **Washing:** cleanse with a **residue-free** shampoo so buildup doesn't cloud the locs;
  dry **completely** (locs hold water and can mildew).
- **Moisture:** a light water-and-oil mist keeps locs supple, not greasy.

### Wigs
- **Storage:** keep on a **wig stand** or in a satin bag — never balled up — to hold the shape.
- **Edges & lace:** remove adhesive gently; cleanse the lace so it lasts and lies flat.
- **Washing:** wash every **8–10 wears** (or when product builds up) with cool water and
  gentle shampoo; air-dry on the stand.
- **Heat:** use low heat on human-hair units; avoid heat on synthetic unless heat-friendly.
- **Your own hair underneath:** keep it braided down, moisturised and give it a breather
  between installs.

> *In plain English: satin at night, gentle washing, light moisture, and don't keep a style
> in past its best. That's 90% of keeping hair healthy and the style looking fresh.*
