## 2. Where Venture I stands

**In place.** Brand and website live and on-theme · real pricing captured for six service categories · the operating documents (compliance, privacy, pricing, booking, consultation & aftercare, money & direction) written · client printables ready · procurement register built.

**Still open at the venture.** Insurance and ICO registration · a working booking link · real photos · the remaining price categories (locs & retwists, box braids, kids, wig install & revamp, add-ons) · deposit amounts · the studio's contact email and address.

**The edge.** Most braiders compete on price and speed. Hagiazo Hair competes on how the chair *feels* — therapist-trained, restorative care. That is the group's signature too: every future venture should be able to say what its "chair" is.

## 3. The principle the house runs on

**Prove, then build.** Make Venture I undeniable — full books, real reviews, a waitlist — before formalising the group. Focus stays on Hair until a real trigger hits: **Venture II goes live, or the group takes investment.** Until then, lean is right.

## 5. How the house is organised

- **The Group** — vision, brand system, trademark, this brief, and the Venture Charter every new venture starts from.
- **Each venture** — its own operating pack (summary, compliance, privacy, pricing, booking, aftercare, money), its client tools and its procurement. Hagiazo Hair's is complete; Homes and the envisioned ventures hold a charter, ready to be filled.
- **The rule:** a venture inherits the house (name, ethos, identity, standards) and tailors the rest to its world. Nothing in the group layer is rewritten for one venture.

## 6. The spearheads' rhythm

- **Quarterly:** reread this brief; update the house table; close or add decisions.
- **Per venture:** the venture's own pack carries the day-to-day — this brief never duplicates it.
- **On a trigger:** Venture II live, or investment — then formalise Hagiazo Holdings and revisit the full group operating system.

> *In plain English: run Hair brilliantly, protect the name now, and let the house grow one proven venture at a time.*
